Syed Shaheer Abbas Rizvi graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering at Habib University as valedictorian of the Class of 2026 and won the Dean's Medal in Electrical Engineering. His academic interests are at the intersection of state estimation, sensor fusion, perception, learning, and control in mobile robots; how a robot learns what is around it, what it can do next, and how it can go about it, so that it can execute complex tasks in challenging environments. Outside of academics, he enjoys exploring music across diverse genres, watching cricket, playing chess, and discovering indie horror games.

Syed Muhammad Ghufran Alavi is a 2026 graduate in BS Electrical Engineering from Habib University, having also completed an applied sciences semester at the University of British Columbia. He completed his A-Levels from Beaconhouse PECHS Campus, securing a silver medal for his academic excellence. Ghufran is an avid advocate for interdisciplinary engineering and always makes time for collaborative projects that transcend a single degree. His love for innovative technology is also the reason why he enjoys studying robotics, digital twinning, computer vision, and smart power systems. His recent endeavour into localized waste management research with the Sheri Youth Organization has piqued his interest in bridging the gap between academic papers and real-world implementation. Ghufran views the democratization of technology as a guiding principle in his work. He believes that in the local context, tech must reach every household to truly uplift the lifestyles of the community. While theoretical research drives innovation, he holds that its true value is only realized when it serves the people on the ground. This perspective informs his approach to engineering and social entrepreneurship, leading him to initiate multiple startups that empowers high-potential skilled workers to sustain their livelihoods. 
As he transitions into the professional field, he is eager to further refine his technical expertise and continue developing engineering solutions that create a tangible impact.

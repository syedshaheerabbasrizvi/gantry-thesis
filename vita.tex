\textbf{Hamad Asif Khan} is a graduate in BS Computer Engineering from Habib University, where he was a top performer and received the Dean's Medal in Computer Engineering. His interests lie at the intersection of computer vision, robotics, embedded systems, and hardware design, with a focus on building intelligent systems that connect perception, computation, and physical action.
His undergraduate projects span autonomous robotics, industrial computer vision, UAVs, and integrated circuit design. He has designed a power-efficient CMOS buffer for high-speed chiplet interconnects using the Sky130 PDK, and has worked on vision-guided manipulation, multispectral agricultural imaging, and digital hardware design.
Hamad is particularly interested in automation and the development of end-to-end engineering systems to solve practical problems. Outside engineering, he enjoys mathematics, reading, and exploring technology. \newline
